# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Women 20–35, Indonesian, fashion-conscious and value-seeking. They browse on mobile and desktop, looking for preloved fashion and beauty items that feel curated and trustworthy — not like sifting through a cluttered marketplace. They have aesthetic sensibilities and want to feel good about the quality and condition of what they're buying. Shopping here is partly aspirational, partly practical.

## Product Purpose

Cyra is a curated preloved fashion and beauty marketplace in Indonesia. The curation is the differentiator — not just a listing of items, but a trusted edit of quality preloved pieces. Success means a buyer completing a purchase feeling confident about condition, authenticity, and value, and returning because the experience felt personal rather than transactional.

Backend is Strapi CMS; frontend is Next.js 16 with Tailwind v4 and shadcn/ui. Language is Indonesian throughout.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, aspirational. Think: a knowledgeable friend with great taste who curates beautiful preloved pieces for you. The tagline — "Preloved Beauty Terkurasi" — should feel earned by every page, not just stated in the header. Formal but friendly (Anda, not kamu). No hype language. No English mixing.

## Anti-references

- **Tokopedia / Shopee:** Avoid the utilitarian grid, aggressive discount callouts, cluttered UI, and price-primary hierarchy. Cyra is the opposite of a mass marketplace — it's an edit, not a catalog.
- **Generic AI-generated warm cream e-commerce:** The FFFBF7 background and D4A373 primary already risk reading as the 2025 AI-slop aesthetic. Warmth must be carried by typography, imagery, and curation, not defaulted through near-beige surfaces.

## Design Principles

1. **Curation, not catalog.** Every design decision should reinforce that Cyra is a carefully selected edit. Empty states, section headers, and copy should signal taste and restraint — not volume.
2. **Trust through warmth.** Preloved shopping requires overcoming hesitation about condition, authenticity, and seller reliability. Visual warmth, clear condition badges, and confident photography hierarchy build this trust.
3. **Indonesian by default.** The experience is built for Indonesian women, in Indonesian. Cultural context and aesthetic cues should feel native, not translated from a Western e-commerce template.
4. **Discovery over search.** Cyra shoppers are often browsing and discovering, not hunting for a specific SKU. Prioritize category exploration, editorial moments, and visual delight over utility-first IA.
5. **Condition transparency.** The preloved context makes product detail pages the most trust-critical surface. Images, condition descriptions, and specifications should feel primary — not an afterthought below a big CTA.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target: 4.5:1 contrast for body text, keyboard-navigable interactions, screen-reader friendly structure. Reduced motion already handled in globals.css. No formal certification required, but AA compliance is the floor.
